# Prep Doc — Session Planning Questionnaire (couples / engagement / pre-wedding)

Date: 25 August 2026
Piece: Customer-facing questionnaire, sent immediately after booking confirmation. Reusable for every session booking.
Protocol: Customer-Facing Copy Protocol applies. Source template READ IN FULL before drafting (Maddie Mae "Create - Elopement Initial Planning Questionnaire Template").

---

## Purpose

Gather everything needed to design and craft a session before the shoot, so the couple never has to figure out logistics themselves. Per Hadi (25 Aug): outfit, vision for the day, everything they have in mind, their relationship, how they met. First post-booking step — the questionnaire replaces a planning call as the default.

## Source material and how it was used

**Maddie Mae Initial Planning Questionnaire (knowledge base, read 25 Aug):** architecture adopted wholesale — (1) relationship/story block first, (2) the-day block second, (3) logistics/preferences/dislikes last, (4) sent right after retainer payment, (5) explicit "what you really DON'T like" question, (6) permission-giving framing so couples answer honestly rather than performatively.

**What was deliberately NOT carried over:** her elopement-adventure specifics (hiking ability, 4WD/OHV road experience, altitude intolerance, camping, sunrise-vs-sunset multi-day logistics, guest mobility for remote ceremony sites). Wrong context for a 90-minute styled London street session.

**Brand foundation inputs:**
- Refined Dreamer voice (locked 4 June) — warm, unhurried, never clipped or form-like
- Prompt-not-pose philosophy (locked 12 June): the questionnaire must MINE for the emotional material Hadi uses to prompt on the day (stories, inside jokes, what makes them laugh). This is the mechanism, not decoration.
- Guidance layer: the questionnaire itself should feel like being looked after
- No em dashes. No cliché crutch words.
- Nervousness-to-ease insight (25 client reviews): include a low-pressure question about camera comfort so Hadi knows in advance who needs warming up.

**Live-client driver:** Selina & Marli, 6 Sept — Bond-leading mood with In the Mood for Love traces, one outfit, images needed for a wedding website. Their case proves the questionnaire needs: outfit detail, mood/reference capture, intended image use, and must-have shot requests.

## What this piece must NOT do

- Not read as an interrogation or an intake form. Warm, curious, human.
- Not overwhelm. Optional questions marked optional; no minimum answer length demanded.
- Not ask what the booking already answered (date, collection, price).
- Not use adventure-elopement vocabulary. Not use banned crutch words as filler.
- Not promise a call as default (consult-tiering decision, 23 Aug).

## What good looks like

The couple finishes it feeling more excited than when they started, and slightly seen. Hadi opens the answers and can immediately design a route, anticipate the outfit and light, know which prompts will land on the day, and know what to avoid. Nothing about the session has to be improvised.

## Structure

1. **The two of you** — how they met, what they love, a story that sums them up, inside jokes, how they are with a camera
2. **Your session** — mood/vision, references, what the images are for, must-haves, meaningful places
3. **The practical part** — outfits, arrival, mobility/comfort, timing constraints, anything to avoid
4. **Anything else** — open field
