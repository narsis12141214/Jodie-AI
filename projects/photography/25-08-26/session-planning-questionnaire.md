# Session Planning Questionnaire
## Hadi Photography London — canonical copy for couples / engagement / pre-wedding sessions

Send: immediately after booking confirmation (first post-booking step).
Build in: Dubsado / Google Forms / Typeform. Wording below is canonical.
Voice: Refined Dreamer. No em dashes.

---

### INTRO (top of form)

Now the good part.

Everything you write here goes straight into how your session is designed: where we go, when we start, what we do, and how it feels. The more you share, the more the day becomes yours rather than a set of photographs that could have been taken of anyone.

There are no wrong answers, and nothing here needs to be polished. Write as much or as little as you like.

---

### PART ONE — THE TWO OF YOU

**How did you two meet?**
Tell it the way you would tell a friend. The version with the detail you always include.

**What do you love most about each other?**
Answer this one separately if you can, each about the other. It is one of the most useful things I ever read.

**Is there a story that sums the two of you up?**
The one that gets retold. The thing that always makes you laugh. An inside joke I would not understand but you both would.

**How do you each feel about being photographed?**
Completely honestly. "Excited", "quietly dreading it", "one of us loves it and one of us does not." Most people say some version of the second one, and knowing in advance changes how I work with you on the day.

**Anything else about the two of you I should know?** *(optional)*
Pets, family, something you are celebrating, something happening in your lives right now.

---

### PART TWO — YOUR SESSION

**What do you picture when you imagine these photographs?**
A mood, a film, a colour, a feeling, a time of day. However it lives in your head is the right way to describe it.

**Do you have references?** *(optional)*
A Pinterest board, saved Instagram posts, film stills, anything. Links or attachments both work.

**What will these photographs be used for?**
Your wedding website, invitations, prints for the wall, sharing with family, or simply having them. This shapes how we shoot and what we prioritise.

**Are there any specific shots you already know you want?**
A particular place, a particular kind of image, something you have always pictured of the two of you.

**Are there places in London that mean something to you?**
Where you met, your first date, the street you always walk, the view you both love. These are often the strongest photographs of the whole session.

**Is there anything you would rather avoid?**
A style you dislike, a kind of photograph that makes you uncomfortable, somewhere you would rather not go, anything at all. Being direct here is genuinely helpful.

---

### PART THREE — THE PRACTICAL PART

**What are you planning to wear?**
Describe it, or send a photo. Colours and fabrics matter more than you would think once we are choosing where to stand.

**Are you bringing anything with you?**
A second outfit, flowers, a jacket for later, a prop that means something.

**Is there anything about comfort or mobility I should plan around?**
Shoes, walking distance, an injury, anything at all. The route is built around you, not the other way round.

**Do you have timing constraints on the day?**
Something before or after, a train to catch, dinner booked. Good to know so we finish calmly rather than in a rush.

**How are you getting to us, and where are you coming from?**
It helps me choose a starting point that works for your journey.

---

### PART FOUR — ANYTHING ELSE

**Anything at all you would like to add?**
Questions, worries, ideas at 2am, things you are unsure about. This box exists for exactly that.

---

### CLOSING LINE (bottom of form)

Thank you. Once this is with me I will start designing your session and will be back to you with the plan.

## Internal notes (not client-facing)

- **The prompt mine:** questions 1-3 in Part One exist so Hadi has real material to prompt with on the day (the father-of-the-bride mechanism: give them something true to respond to, photograph the reaction). Read these before every shoot.
- **Camera-comfort question** is the nervousness-to-ease insight from the 25-review analysis, asked directly and permissively so people answer honestly.
- **"What will these be used for"** came from Selina & Marli (wedding website + invitations) — changes shooting priorities, and nobody thinks to volunteer it.
- **Reusability:** works as-is for couples, engagement, pre-wedding, and anniversary sessions. For weddings, Part Three needs a full timeline/vendor/family-group extension — separate document.
- **Where it sits in the flow:** payment → confirmation → THIS → session crafted from answers → 48h weather + exact time → shoot.
