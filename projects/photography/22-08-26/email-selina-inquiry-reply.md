In the Mood for Love is a wonderful brief to be handed. Almost nothing happens in that film, and everything happens: two people, narrow spaces, lamplight, and all that feeling held in stillness. When we plan your route, we will choose the two or three that fit the mood you are after, and I will time us so the late-afternoon light works with the shadows rather than against them.
