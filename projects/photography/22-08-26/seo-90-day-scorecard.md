# SEO 90-Day Scorecard — Pre-Registered Checkpoints
Committed: 22 August 2026
Baseline week: w/e 19 Aug 2026 — 11 clicks/wk, 2,861 impressions/wk, CTR 0.38%, ~91% of clicks on homepage, high volatility (20+ queries swinging >10 places/week), referring domains baseline to be captured in week 1
Purpose: these predictions are written BEFORE execution so the methodology can be judged against them, not against vibes. If we hit the execution marks and miss the indicator marks, the METHODOLOGY gets formally reviewed — we do not silently re-run it.

---

## The contract

**Jodie owns:** every brief, every outreach draft, every venue-page prep doc, weekly anomaly checks, monthly scorecard reads, keeping this cadence alive even when other work is loud.
**Hadi owns:** the actions only he can do — publishing pages in Showit, GBP updates, review asks at shoots, sending outreach emails, supplying venue photos/stories.
**The rule:** indicator misses only count against the methodology if the execution column was actually done. Execution misses are a different failure with a different fix.

## 30-day checkpoint (~21 September 2026)

Execution (binary, all Hadi+Jodie):
- [ ] Migration forensics complete: old-URL inventory crawled, redirect repairs applied, canonical/hostname audit done
- [ ] Both sitemaps (Showit + WordPress) verified valid and submitted
- [ ] Branded-query artifact check completed (30-min checklist)
- [ ] GBP completeness pass done: category, services, 50+ photos, weekly upload habit started
- [ ] Review velocity restarted: first new review requests out (Steva shoot = first natural ask, 10 Oct)
- [ ] First 4 venue pages briefed, written, published
- [ ] Venue/vendor link-ask list built (every venue shot in 10 years) + first 5 asks sent

Expected indicators at 30 days (leading only — clicks NOT expected to move yet):
- Venue pages indexed within 2 weeks of publish
- No indexation/canonical errors outstanding in GSC
- Referring domains baseline captured, first 1-2 new ones in flight

## 60-day checkpoint (~21 October 2026)

Execution:
- [ ] 8-12 venue pages live total, sustained 1-2/week cadence held
- [ ] 15+ link asks sent (venues, real-wedding submissions to 3+ wedding blogs, 1 styled-shoot conversation started)
- [ ] GBP photo count 100+, review count growing (target: +3-5 new reviews)

Expected indicators:
- Venue pages registering impressions on "[venue] wedding photographer/photos" queries
- 3+ new referring domains vs baseline
- Volatility declining: count of >10-place swings on 10+ impression queries trending down vs August
- GBP actions (calls, website taps, direction requests) up vs September baseline

## 90-day checkpoint (~21 November 2026)

Execution:
- [ ] 15-20 venue pages live, cadence never broke for more than 2 consecutive weeks
- [ ] 5+ acquired links/mentions (editorial or venue), 100 reviews → 105-110 with recent dates

Expected indicators:
- At least 2-3 venue pages holding positions 1-20 on their venue query (venue terms are low-KD; this is the winnability bet in action)
- Non-brand clicks trending above the 11/week baseline (direction matters more than magnitude at this point)
- Volatility measurably lower than August baseline
- Impressions on target topics up; homepage click-share below 85% (interior pages waking up)

---

## Review triggers (pre-committed, no re-litigating later)

1. **90-day full review regardless of outcome** — scorecard read line by line, honest verdict written.
2. **Execution done + indicators flat = methodology review** — formally revisit the playbook with fresh research; do not silently re-run the same plan.
3. **Execution not done = execution review** — the methodology is unjudged; fix the doing, not the plan.
4. **Any confirmed (non-artifact) branded-query loss or indexation break at any point = immediate escalation**, outside the 90-day cycle.

## What is NOT promised
Rankings and clicks are not promised — nobody honest promises them (only ~2-6% of new pages reach top 10 within a year; Google is not ours to control). What is promised: every action traceable to evidence, leading indicators that reveal within 60-90 days whether it's working, and pre-registered checkpoints that make failure loud and early instead of quiet and late.
