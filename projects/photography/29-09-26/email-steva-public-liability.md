## Before you send — two things

1. **Have the certificate to hand.** The email promises it today. Most insurers let you download the schedule as a PDF from your account in about a minute. Saying yes and then not sending it is worse than not offering.

2. **⚠️ Check your cover level against what the council asks for.** This is the only real risk in an otherwise simple reply. Councils set a minimum for park permits and it is often higher than a standard policy. If your cover sits below what Bexleyheath requires, the permit could be refused with the shoot 11 days away. **I do not know your cover amount or their requirement, so I am not guessing at either.** Worth two minutes on the certificate before this goes out. If there is a shortfall, most insurers can raise the limit same day.
