Hi Selina, hi Marli,

I have read your answers twice, and I want to start with the thing I keep coming back to. Marli turning up on time to a party in California where nobody does, sitting on the couch for an hour while Selina cooked, and then four days in a row where it felt strange that nothing felt strange. That is the whole thing, really. Everything since, including a week of not talking and passing notes through friends anyway, is just the two of you being consistent.

**Selina, first.** Thank you for telling me you are dreading it a bit. Most people feel some version of that and very few say it out loud, and knowing changes how I work with you.

So here is how Saturday actually goes. I will not stand you in front of me and ask you to pose. I do not do that, and it is where self-conscious photographs come from. What I do instead is give you two something to do or something to talk about, and then I photograph what happens between you. You will spend most of the session looking at each other rather than at me. The pictures you will love most will be ones you did not know I was taking. And if there are angles you know you dislike, tell me on the day, or point at them, or just say "not that one." I would much rather know.

**The plan.** Westminster, and the river.

You asked for Parliament and Big Ben from across the water, with the fish lampposts. What struck me is that this is the same stretch of river Selina described as the place you first stood together and decided London could be home. Your dream shot and your meaningful place are the same view. We are building the session around that.

It gives us everything you asked for: the grand, classic, slightly cinematic London of the Bond films, all of it old and architectural rather than commercial, and firmly on the right side of town. Stone, water, iron, and the light coming off the Thames late in the day.

And Marli, it is built around you. Short distances between everything, plenty of shade, places to stop and sit whenever you want one. Nothing about Saturday requires walking far.

**On the heat.** I will check the forecast on Thursday and confirm your exact start time then. If it looks warm we simply start later and let the light come to us, which suits the mood we are making anyway. Please do not worry about this. It is my job to manage, not yours.

**What we will make.** Both of the things you each described. The glamorous, styled, slightly mysterious frames Selina pictured, and the ones Marli asked for where you are laughing and looking at each other. The second kind is what makes the first kind work.

**Your outfits, and a few notes worth having in advance.**

First, they work beautifully together. Deep green velvet against ivory lace is a rich, classic pairing, and the two fabrics behave in lovely opposite ways in low light. Velvet drinks light and goes deep and soft. Lace catches it and glows. Against Portland stone and the river at that hour, you will look exactly like the reference images you sent.

A few practical things:

- **Selina, the lace is delicate and slightly sheer**, which at golden hour is genuinely beautiful, because low sun behind you turns fine lace luminous. It is also worth knowing in advance so it is your choice rather than a surprise. Wear whatever underlayer you would feel most comfortable in with the light behind you, ideally seamless and close to your skin tone. Then we can use backlight freely and you never have to think about it.
- **Steam both pieces the night before** and hang them, rather than folding them into a bag. Velvet holds creases stubbornly and lace shows every fold, and both photograph.
- **Marli, no tie is a good choice** and reads as the relaxed end of the look you are after. If you happen to own a black bow tie, bring it in a pocket. It costs nothing and gives us two distinct moods in one outfit: collar open for the warmer frames, tie on for the sharper ones.
- **Hands.** You are newly engaged and there will be close frames of the two of you together, so a quick look at nails the day before is worth it. Nothing elaborate.
- **Selina, bring the spare shoes**, exactly as you suggested. Heels for the photographs, comfortable pair for walking between them.
- **Water for both of you**, and Marli, a bottle in my bag as well. We will have shade and places to sit throughout.

That is Saturday. Come as you are, expect to talk more than you pose, and leave the rest to me.

I will be in touch Thursday with your exact start time and where to meet.

Warmly,
Hadi
