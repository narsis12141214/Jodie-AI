## 1. Contract paragraph — v2, ORIGINAL LANGUAGE (for Hadi to paste into the agreement)

v1 (below, superseded) tracked the videographer's sentence structure too closely. v2 is written from scratch in Hadi Photography's own register: leads with what the couple receives, plain language, positive framing, same legal substance.

> **Your Photographs**
>
> From the moment your final gallery is delivered and your balance is settled, your photographs are yours to enjoy for life. Print them, frame them, build your wedding website with them, post them, send them with your invitations, share them with anyone you choose, anywhere in the world, for as long as you like. Every image arrives clean, with no watermark, and you will never need to ask permission or pay anything further to use your photographs in your own life.
>
> Hadi Photography London remains the author and copyright holder of the work, and may feature a selection of images in its portfolio, website, and marketing. If you would rather keep some or all of your photographs private, tell us in writing at any time and we will honour that fully.
>
> The one thing we ask: if a photograph is ever wanted for commercial use by a brand, publication, or any third party, speak to us first. Written approval from Hadi Photography is needed for those uses, and it is not unreasonably withheld.

**Why this version:**
- Zero structural resemblance to the videographer clause — no "perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive license to view, download, store..." verb-chain. It reads like Hadi Photography wrote it, because it did.
- Legal substance intact: grant trigger (delivery + full payment), unlimited personal use in perpetuity worldwide, no watermark, copyright retained, portfolio right with written privacy opt-out, third-party commercial use by consent ("not unreasonably withheld" keeps it friendly and enforceable).
- Positive framing throughout per the 8 Aug rule: even the commercial boundary is "speak to us first," a courtesy, not a prohibition.
- Covers everything Selina asked for and more (her videographer's clause stopped at "personal, non-commercial"; this one names the wedding website and public sharing explicitly).

> **Image License.** Upon full payment of all amounts due under this Agreement, the Client is granted a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive license to view, download, store, copy, print, reproduce, share, and publicly display the final delivered photographs, without watermark and without any further permission or notice required. No additional fee applies to any of these uses, at any time. The Photographer retains copyright in the photographs and the right to feature them in his portfolio, website, and promotional materials, subject to any privacy preference the Client communicates in writing. For commercial resale or sublicensing of the photographs to third parties, the Photographer's prior written consent is required.
