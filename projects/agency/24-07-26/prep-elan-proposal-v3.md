## Why this is a rebuild, not a revision

v1 and v2 sold the wrong thing. They led with product ("Ella, your AI voice agent") when the actual thing being bought is a full custom-designed system that ends a broken, unscalable manual workflow. Hadi's clarification on 24 July was the key correction:

> "This is the main thing: they can't scale it up this way if they want to expand their business. They asked if I can create an agent, and then I thought about it and came up with this great idea to have a page where customers can create a bespoke cake design, finalise it, see how it looks, pay for it. That's it: no more paperwork, no more going back and forth. They are not paying for a beautiful ordering page. It's way beyond that. We've designed a whole system for them."

So the proposal has to do three new things:
1. Open with EL&N's own current pain — not with our product name. The reader must recognise their own operation in the first two paragraphs before Click's name appears.
2. Frame Ella + the cake designer + Click Pro + Stripe as a **single custom-designed system**, not four features.
3. Rename and reframe the upfront fee. "Setup fee" reads as configuration cost and makes a 5-figure number feel unjustified. This is a paid custom system that Hadi conceived, designed and built — the pricing language must reflect that.

Research is being run in parallel on pricing terminology + benchmarks + how bespoke agencies frame this. Investment section finalised on those findings.

## Purpose

Win Elan Cafe's signature on the custom cake-ordering system for the London branch on a 12-month partnership, with pricing that reflects the value of a bespoke system Hadi designed for them from scratch, in an EL&N-branded proposal that Ali Bidarbakht can circulate internally without translation.

## Locked foundation pieces that apply

- Positioning: done-for-you AI Operations Partner — sell the team and the service depth, not the tool (decisions.md 2026-05-13).
- Setup and monthly must read as visibly different numbers (decisions.md 2026-05-13, permanent principle). Still applies at the new upfront number.
- Never "free" or "bonus" in pricing. Any concession has a reason and a limit.
- Payment milestones tied to objective events, never satisfaction conditions.
- No em dashes anywhere.

## Source material (Hadi's briefs, 23-24 July)

- Current pain: "They were struggling with paperwork and working weekdays. They were struggling to reply back to clients through email because their current system is old and manual. Customers go to their website, fill out a very complicated form, and once they receive the order, they have to reply back by email. Sometimes, just going back and forth with a customer requires over 10 to 15 emails, and this is really time-consuming, complicated, and not scalable."
- Scale ask: "They can't scale it up this way if they want to expand their business."
- The idea and its origin: "They asked if I can create an agent, and then I thought about it and came up with this great idea to have a page where customers can create a bespoke cake design, finalise it, see how it looks, pay for it. That's it: everything, no more paperwork, no more going back and forth with customers."
- The outcome: "Once the order is paid for, the kitchen would receive a copy of the order along with an image so they can start making the cake. It's that simple: no more course will be missed, no more paperwork, no more humans involved. Everything is going to be automated, and it's easily scalable. They can scale it to 200 orders per day, that simple."
- On pricing: "As for the setup fee, this is the main thing. I don't want to only mention setup fee, because they may not understand why they charge £5,000, £6,000, or £7,000 for the setup. It doesn't make sense, so we have to remove setup fee. The fee that we charge is for the ideation and the idea of creating this page, because this was basically my idea. The ideation, implementation, and the whole thing came from me... they're paying for the whole system. We've designed a whole system for them... maybe we have to slightly raise the price here and give it some room, so if they want to negotiate some sort of discount, we can reduce the amount a little bit."
- Marketing team USA-branch comment (already handled in v2): the per-branch deployment framing and IP/licence clause stays.
- All 8 v2 adjustments stay: 24/7 + concurrent answering, Stripe payout every 2-3 days, kitchen order image, dedicated account manager, thorough monthly report, £0.25/min overage, 85% usage reminder, no roadmap section.

## New structure (v3)

1. **The problem you told us about.** Two short paragraphs in EL&N's operational language: complicated form, 10-15 emails per order, admin drag, doesn't scale for expansion.
2. **The system we designed for you.** Names all four components as one system: voice agent (Ella), bespoke cake designer page, Stripe payment, kitchen delivery via Click Pro. Frames this as a custom design work, not an off-the-shelf install.
3. **How it works in practice.** The 5-step customer journey (from v2 section 2, all improvements retained).
4. **What ends when Ella goes live.** Explicit before/after list: paperwork ends, email threads end, missed orders end, weekend admin ends, hard scaling limit ends. This is the "paint the picture" section Hadi asked for.
5. **Reliability and support.** v2 section retained verbatim (monitoring, thorough report, 10 menu updates/mo at 48hr, dedicated account manager, quarterly reviews, trusted infrastructure).
6. **Built once, deployed per branch.** v2 section retained (per-branch deployment + IP/licence clause — silent answer to the USA reuse comment).
7. **Investment.** Fee renamed (research-informed), price bumped with negotiation buffer, £500/mo unchanged, £0.25/min overage + 85% alert retained, payment schedule adjusted.
8. **Partnership terms.** 12-month + quarterly reviews + expansion commitment.
9. **Next step.** Confirmation → agreement issued → go-live prep for 1 August.

## Visual direction (EL&N palette)

From the EL&N London site screenshot:

- Header/hero band: dusty blush pink (approx #E5CBC0)
- Body: warm cream/off-white (approx #FAF5F0)
- Primary text: near-black charcoal (approx #2C2C2C)
- Accents / dividers / section markers: rose (approx #E5B7B7) or deeper blush
- Secondary/subtle backgrounds: soft pink (approx #F5DDE0)
- Typography feel: clean sans-serif, generous whitespace, high-end retail minimalism, letter-spaced eyebrow labels

Design intent: the proposal should feel like it belongs in EL&N's brand world when Ali forwards it internally, not like a generic agency deck. That's a trust signal on its own.

## What this must NOT do

- No opening with "Ella" or "Click." Open with EL&N's problem in their own operational language.
- No "setup fee" anywhere. The fee is for the system Hadi designed.
- No overselling: no invented uptime %, no ROI projections we can't defend.
- No em dashes, no cliché-generic language, no jargon.
- No hidden pricing math. Overage, alerts, and payment schedule are all in the document.

The document feels like it was made for EL&N specifically, because the palette and pacing come from their brand.
