## 1. The problem you told us about

Today, a customer wanting a bespoke cake fills out a long form on your website, and from that moment your team is on the clock. Ten to fifteen emails to confirm the design, the flavours, the message, the collection details, the payment. Multiplied across every order, every day of the week. Weekends and evenings do not stop; the inbox does not stop.

It is careful, personal work, and it is why EL&N's cakes matter. It is also work that quietly caps how far the department can grow. Every new order is another manual thread. Doubling orders means doubling admin. Opening another branch means opening another admin operation from scratch. It does not scale.

## 2. The system we designed for you

We designed a complete, custom ordering system for the EL&N cake department. Not a plug-in, not an off-the-shelf tool. A system built specifically for how your customers order and how your kitchen delivers.

It has four parts, working as one:

- **Ella**, a dedicated AI voice agent that answers your cake ordering line 24 hours a day, seven days a week, and takes orders end to end.
- **A bespoke cake designer page**, custom-built for EL&N, where customers with a specific vision build and visualise their cake step by step and see it before they commit.
- **Stripe payment**, integrated into both flows, with funds paid out to EL&N every two to three days.
- **Click Pro**, your order management dashboard, where every completed order arrives ready for the kitchen with an image of the cake and the full spec in one place.

Ella, the designer page, the payment layer and the dashboard were conceived, designed and built together to solve one problem: turning cake enquiries into paid, kitchen-ready orders without a single email in between.

## 3. How it works in practice

When a customer calls the cake ordering line:

1. Ella answers immediately, every time, 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Even when two or three customers call at the same time, every call is picked up. No busy line, no waiting, no missed order.
2. Standard cakes: Ella takes the cake selection from your current range, the customer's name and contact details, and sends a secure payment link.
3. Custom cakes: Ella sends the customer a link to EL&N's bespoke cake designer, where they build their cake step by step and watch the design come to life as they choose.
4. Payment is taken through Stripe, with funds paid out to EL&N every two to three days. Card details are handled entirely by Stripe and never touch our systems or yours.
5. Every completed order lands in Click Pro, ready for the kitchen: an image of the order and exactly what the customer chose and agreed, all in one place. No paperwork, no notes passed back and forth.

## 4. What ends when the system goes live
