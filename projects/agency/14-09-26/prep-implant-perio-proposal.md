## 6. Proposed structure — FOR HADI TO APPROVE OR CORRECT

### One-off: Avatar Studio Setup
Creating the digital twins is a bespoke build, so it carries its own fee, visibly distinct from the monthly.
- Studio session at the clinic: avatar capture for **2 clinicians** (Dr Negahban + Dr Tehrani; Dr Tinti as an optional third), voice capture, consent + likeness agreement
- Brand kit for video: lower-thirds, end cards, caption style, colour grade matched to #606c68 / #232737
- Content pillars + 90-day editorial plan (implants, perio, Invisalign, whitening, patient journey, team, clinic)
- Foundation photography session: clinic, team, treatment rooms, hero images for the website
- Instagram profile rebuild: bio, highlights, link, grid strategy
- **Suggested: £1,950** (third avatar +£450)

### Option 1 — PRESENCE
*The steady cadence. The clinic shows up every week, in its own voice, without anyone facing a camera.*
- **2 AI avatar reels per week (8 per month)**, scripted by us, approved by the clinician before production
- **1 additional post per week** (carousel, photo, or educational static), so **3 posts per week / 12 per month**
- Stories: 3 per week from the same material
- Instagram fully managed: captions, hashtags, scheduling, comment and DM triage with escalation
- **Quarterly photography session** (half day) refreshing website and social imagery
- Monthly performance report
- Named account manager, same-business-day response
- **Suggested: £1,490/month**

### Option 2 — AUTHORITY
*The full engine. Enough volume to own the specialist conversation in London, and the assets to match on the website.*
- **4 AI avatar reels per week (16 per month)**
- **2 additional posts per week**, so **6 posts per week / 24 per month**
- Stories: daily
- Instagram AND Facebook managed (their FB is the only social they currently link)
- **Monthly photography session** (half day), including consented patient-journey and before/after work where the clinic supplies consent
- Website video: one 60-90 second avatar-led explainer per month for a treatment page (implants, perio, Invisalign, rotating)
- Monthly strategy call + report, quarterly review
- **Dedicated account manager**
- **Suggested: £2,490/month**

### Terms (LaMure precedent for premium)
- 12-month partnership, then rolling, 30 days' notice
- Studio Setup on signing, first month in advance
- Every script approved by the named clinician before production. **Nothing is published in their likeness that they have not signed off in writing.** This is the compliance backbone and it is also the sales line.

### Why these numbers
- Both land inside the UK mid-tier band (£1,500-£4,000) for a 3-clinician specialist practice, with Option 2 mid-band. Defensible against any competing quote.
- Economics hold: Option 2 is ~30 hrs/month of production at ~£80/hr gross after ~£150/mo tooling. Option 1 ~18 hrs at the same rate. Consistent, not invented.
- ~£30k/year for Option 2 against a business that retained £238k in one entity last year and spent £331k acquiring. Comfortable.
- **Ceiling if Hadi wants to push: £2,990 for Option 2.** Still mid-band. The argument against: this is our first dental AI-avatar deployment and the case study is worth more than £500/mo.

## 7. What this piece must NOT do
- Quote or hint at their financial position. Research informs us, never appears.
- Mention Hadi Photography London, wedding work, or any photography brand. "Our team provides professional photography."
- Claim the AI content is hands-off or unreviewed. Human-reviewed, clinician-approved, always.
- Over-promise follower counts or lead numbers. State what we produce and how it is measured.
- Use "free," "bonus," "no," "not included," "excluded."
- Use "setup fee" for the avatar build. It is a bespoke build.
- Lead with price. Lead with the gap (premium clinic, invisible presence) and the mechanism (their expertise, without the camera).
- Skip regulatory awareness. Dental advertising sits under GDC guidance and the CAP Code; scripts must be accurate and non-misleading, before/after needs patient consent. One calm line that we build this in is a differentiator against generic agencies.
