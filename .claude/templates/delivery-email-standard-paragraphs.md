# Standard paragraphs for delivery emails
**Use:** Every final-gallery email (photography) and every month-end / milestone email (agency) carries BOTH of these. Not diarised separately, because diarised asks never fire (review ask sat in priorities from 25 Aug to 14 Sept, never sent).
**Voice:** Refined Dreamer for photography. Direct-warm for agency. No em dashes.

---

## Photography — final gallery email

### Review ask (goes after the gallery link, before the sign-off)
> One small thing, and only if the photographs are what you hoped for. A few honest lines on Google is how the next couple finds me, and it matters more than anything I could say about myself. Here is the link: [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]. Two minutes, whenever you like.

### Referral ask (one sentence, after the review ask)
> And if you know anyone planning a day in London, wedding, elopement, engagement or just the two of them, I would be glad to meet them.

---

## Agency — month-end or milestone email

### Check-in (opens the email)
> Two questions before the numbers. What is working? What is not? Straight answers help more than polite ones.

### Referral ask (closes the email)
> If you know another [restaurant / clinic / salon] owner who is losing calls the way you were, I would like to meet them. One introduction is worth more to me than any advert.

---

## Rules
- Review ask only after the client has SEEN the work and had a chance to react. Never in the same email as an invoice.
- Referral ask is one sentence. No scheme, no discount, no incentive. Just the question.
- If the delivery had a problem, the review ask is removed from that email and returns after the problem is fixed.
