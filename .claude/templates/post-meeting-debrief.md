# Post-meeting debrief
**Use:** Straight after ANY prospect or client meeting, before Jodie drafts a single word. Voice note or typed. Five minutes.
**Rule:** Jodie drafts from this, not from memory. Where the debrief and a later summary disagree, the debrief wins.
**Why this exists:** Implant + Perio Clinic, 14 Sept 2026. A trusted-friend referral received a cold-lead proposal built on a verbal summary ("they love the idea") that turned out not to match what she said. Lost.

---

**Meeting:** [who, where, date]

**1. How did this lead reach us?**
Cold / inbound (which channel) / warm referral (from whom, how close) / repeat client / other.
→ This decides the play. Warm referral = short play (small ask, fast yes, minimal document). Cold = full proposal.

**2. What did they say they want? THEIR WORDS.**
Quote them. Not "they want content." Write "she said: we need help with photography and video for the clinic."

**3. What did I propose in the room, and how did they react? THEIR WORDS.**
"I suggested X. She said: ___." If the reaction was a nod or "sounds interesting," write that, not "they loved it."

**4. What constraints did they state?**
Budget signals. Time. Staff. Camera comfort. Existing supplier and when that ends. Anything they said they do NOT want.

**5. Who else decides? Have I met them?**
Partners, spouses, practice managers, finance. If the answer is "I don't know," the proposal waits until we do (deals over £1,000/month).

**6. What did we agree happens next, and by when?**
The exact next step in their words if possible. "She said send me something by Friday."

**7. My confidence they will buy, 1 to 5, and the one reason.**

**8. Anything I said that I should not have, or anything I promised?**
Prices mentioned. Timelines. Freebies. Names dropped.
