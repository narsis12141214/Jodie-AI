# Inquiry follow-up playbook — Hadi Photography London
**Built 29 September 2026**, after Arijit & Raima (said yes, never paid, never followed up) and Michael & Carly (fixed travel dates, phone trigger never fired). Both lost to silence on our side, not theirs.
**This is pre-mortem item 4.2**: "a cadence that is not enforced is a wish."

**Source:** Maddie Mae Inquiry-to-Booking follow-up templates (3/7/14 day cadence, same thread, same subject line) + Hadi's instruction 29 Sept ("after 2 days, if we didn't receive anything back, a follow-up") + the two live losses.

⚠️ **Templates below are DRAFTS awaiting Hadi's green light.** Nothing sends until he approves the wording.

---

## The one mechanical rule that applies to every touch

**Always reply in the SAME email thread with the SAME subject line.** Never start a new chain. This pushes the original email, with all its detail, back to the top of their inbox. A new subject line buries it.

---

## Three tracks. Pick by the date, not by the person.

| Track | When it applies | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Standard** | Shoot or wedding date more than 3 weeks away | Day 2 email → Day 5 WhatsApp → Day 9 email → Day 14 close-out |
| **B — Urgent** | Date within 3 weeks, OR client is travelling on fixed dates | Hour 0 email + WhatsApp together → Day 1 WhatsApp → Day 2 phone call → Day 4 close-out |
| **C — Awaiting payment** | They have said YES and the booking link is out | Hour 4 WhatsApp → Day 1 phone call → Day 2 deadline-and-release |

**Track C is the one that has cost real money.** LaMure (~£20k annualised) and Arijit (£680) both died between "yes" and "paid." A yes decays faster than an inquiry does. Treat it as the most urgent thing on the board, not the safest.

## TRACK A — Standard

### Day 2, email (same thread, same subject)
> Hi [Name],
>
> Making sure this reached you, since email has a habit of burying things.
>
> [Date] is still open and I would like to hold it for you.
>
> Shall I?
>
> Warmly,
> Hadi

### Day 5, WhatsApp
> Hi [Name], Hadi here from Hadi Photography London. I wrote to you about [date] earlier in the week, worth a look in spam if it is not in your inbox. The date is still open. Would you like me to hold it?

### Day 9, email (same thread). Lead with value, not a chase.
> Hi [Name],
>
> A week disappears quickly when you are planning something, so no need to apologise for the quiet.
>
> [ONE USEFUL THING. A note on the light at their time of year, a thought about their location, or a link to the relevant guide. Something they can use whether or not they book me.]
>
> [Date] is still free. Shall I hold it for you?
>
> Warmly,
> Hadi

### Day 14, close-out (same thread)
> Hi [Name],
>
> I am going to close your enquiry so it stops sitting in your inbox, and release [date] back to the diary. Nothing needed from you.
>
> If your plans firm up later, write to me and I will tell you honestly whether the date is still there. I would be glad to hear from you either way.
>
> Wishing you both a wonderful [wedding / year].
>
> Warmly,
> Hadi

## TRACK B — Urgent (date within 3 weeks, or travelling client)

### Hour 0 — email and WhatsApp go out together, always
The WhatsApp confirms the address is right, gets past spam, and reaches someone who is on a trip rather than at a desk. **This is already the rule and it is the one that would have saved Michael & Carly.**

> Hi [Name], Hadi here from Hadi Photography London. I have just replied to your enquiry about [date] by email, worth checking spam if it is not in your inbox. Short version: the date works. Details in the email.

### Day 1, WhatsApp
> Hi [Name], following up on [date]. It is close now so I wanted to make sure you had what you needed. The slot is still yours if you want it. Shall I hold it?

### Day 2, PHONE CALL. Not an email.
Thirty seconds. "Hi [Name], Hadi here, the photographer. Just checking you got my email about [date]. Did you want to go ahead?"
If voicemail: leave one, then WhatsApp saying you called.

### Day 4, close-out
> Hi [Name], I am releasing [date] since it is nearly here and I have not managed to reach you. If something changes, message me any time. Enjoy London either way.

---

## TRACK C — Awaiting payment after a yes

### Hour 4, WhatsApp
> Hi [Name], Hadi here. I have sent the booking link over. If anything about it does not work, tell me and I will sort it another way.

### Day 1, PHONE CALL
The single highest-value call in the business. They have already said yes. Something practical is usually in the way: the link did not open, the card was declined, they are waiting on a partner, they are in a different timezone. All of that is solvable in ninety seconds and unsolvable by email.

### Day 2, deadline and release
> Hi [Name],
>
> I have not managed to reach you and the booking has not come through, so [date] is still unheld.
>
> I will keep it for you until [time tomorrow], then release it back to the diary.
>
> Would you still like it?
>
> Warmly,
> Hadi

A real deadline, honestly stated, is the most respectful thing here. It converts or it closes, and both are better than silence.

## Stop rules
- **Any reply stops the cadence.** Even "we are still deciding." Restart the clock from their message, not from ours.
- **A booking stops the cadence.** Move them out of the tracker into delivery.
- **Close-out is final.** No fifth touch. Per the standing rule: silence after asking is strength.
- **A flagged lead gets no follow-up at all.** Scam markers (see the Andrey pattern, 24 Aug) mean one defended reply and nothing further.

## What never appears in a follow-up
"Just checking in." "No rush." "Totally understand if you're busy." "Whenever you're ready." "Happy to jump on a call." Any em dash. Any pre-booking call offer (locked 21 Sept). Any apology for following up.
